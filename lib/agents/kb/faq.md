## FAQ page extra facts
Source: https://www.carpetshop.co.il/pages/faq
- Visualization: photograph the room or use a built-in photo, choose rug and size, then "הדמיה בחדר שלי". Visualization page: https://www.carpetshop.co.il/pages/visualization-page
- How to open packaging: carefully cut one plastic end with scissors, remove the rug, remove corner protectors, cut and remove tape. Do not use sharp objects on the rug.
- How to lay the rug: spread on the floor and flatten creases by hand or by stepping.
- Spot clean: alcohol-free wipe, or microfiber cloth in warm water and dish soap, circular motion. General clean: professional dry cleaning.
- Anti-slip underlay is recommended under every rug.
- Storage: roll, wrap in nylon, add moth protection, seal with air holes, store dry and shaded.
- Size may vary slightly because of finishing.
- Shedding/fluff on a new natural-fiber rug is normal and should lessen over months with regular vacuuming.
- Custom anti-slip underlay and made-to-order rugs cannot be cancelled.
